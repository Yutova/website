QUICK ANSWERS

A few things that may help.

I forgot my password. What should I do? +

From the Yutova sign-in screen, choose “Forgot password?” and follow the instructions sent to your email address.

Can I delete my account and data? +

Yes. Yutova gives you control over your information. You can permanently delete your account and associated data from within the app.

Are my journal entries used to train AI? +

No. Your journal entries and voice recordings are processed to provide your personal reflections and insights. They are not used to train public or foundational AI models.

Where can I read more about privacy? +

You can read the full Yutova Privacy Policy for details about how your information is handled.

QUESTIONS

A few things you may want to know.

Clear answers about privacy, AI reflections, and how Yutova fits into your life.

Is Yutova therapy? +

No. Yutova is a self-reflection tool and journaling companion, not a therapist, medical provider, or mental healthcare service. It is designed to help you organize thoughts and recognize patterns on your own terms.

How does Yutova use AI? +

Yutova uses language models as a quiet sounding board. When you complete an entry, the AI reads what you wrote to surface thoughtful observations, questions, and patterns—without diagnosing, judging, or telling you what to do.

Are my journal entries private? +

Yes. Your entries belong entirely to you. Your data is encrypted, protected by strict privacy policies, and never shared or sold to third parties.

Are my entries used to train AI? +

No. Your journal entries and voice recordings are processed solely to create reflections and insights for your personal account. They are never used to train public or foundational AI models.

Can I use Yutova without writing every day? +

Yes. Yutova does not have mandatory streaks, guilt notifications, or rigid requirements. You can check in daily, a couple of times a week, or whenever you need space to think.

Can I journal by voice? +

Yes. You can speak naturally into Yutova. The app transcribes your voice entry and generates reflections just as it would with written text, perfect for when typing feels cumbersome.

What's included in Yutova Free? +

Yutova Free gives you unlimited journaling, thoughtful reflections on every entry, limited history.

What does Yutova Pro add? +

Yutova Pro unlocks your full journal history, long-term memory threads, deeper pattern recognition across weeks and months, and comprehensive weekly, monthly, and yearly insights.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time directly through your App Store account settings with no hassle or cancellation fees.

Can I delete or export my data? +

Yes. You have full ownership of your data. You can export your entries anytime or delete your account and all associated data permanently whenever you choose.
